PURPOSE
-------

This runbook is the physical acceptance test for the completed Phase 2 implementation and
the Phase 3 release candidate. Passing the automated checks is required before testing, but
does not prove native permissions, background GPS, map rendering, speech, Bluetooth routing,
or battery behavior.

Phase 3 must not be declared complete until the Android development-build section passes.
Expo Go is useful for foreground behavior only. It cannot certify Kairo's background-location
or foreground-service contract.

The product contract is:

- Kairo owns movement recording and history. There is no Strava connection or upload.
- Movement types are run, walk, and ride.
- Completed activities upload only to Kairo's authenticated backend when sync is configured.
- Normal backgrounding and screen lock must continue recording in a development build.
- Force-kill continuation is not guaranteed. Points persisted before termination must recover.
- Voice cues default on and support independent distance/time toggles.
- Raw GPS points are retained; route trims exclude points and recompute derived metrics.

4. PHASE 2 REGRESSION
---------------------

Initial launch:

1. Confirm the app opens without a blank screen, migration error, or red screen.
2. Confirm these tabs render with icons and readable labels:
   Home, Today, Macros, Workouts, Weight, Movement.
3. Open every tab and return to Home.
4. Confirm headers, tab bar, forms, and bottom buttons do not overlap.

Workouts:

1. Start a workout and add at least two exercises.
2. Log a decimal weight such as `62.5`; also test `62,5` if the device locale uses commas.
3. Background Expo Go briefly, return, and confirm the active workout still displays.
4. Finish the workout and confirm it appears in history and on Home.

Weight:

1. Log `75.5`, or `75,5` with a comma-decimal locale.
2. Confirm history and Home show the same value.
3. Set a goal, restart the app, and confirm the goal persists.
4. Delete a test entry and confirm it disappears after navigation/reload.

Today/tasks:

1. Create a daily task and a selected-weekday task.
2. Complete and clear each task.
3. Confirm streak/rate values change correctly and survive restart.
4. Archive and restore a task.

Macros:

1. Create a food with known nutrition values.
2. Log quantities `1.5` and `0.5`, including comma-decimal input if applicable.
3. Confirm calories and macros scale correctly.
4. Set targets, navigate away, return, and confirm they persist.
5. Delete an entry and confirm day totals recalculate.

Phase 2 native features:

1. Configure a reminder and confirm the permission prompt is understandable.
2. Confirm a scheduled notification is delivered at a near-future test time.
3. Generate a wallpaper, confirm it is nonblank, and test Save to Photos.
4. Confirm denied notification/photo permission produces a recoverable state rather than a crash.


5. EXPO GO MOVEMENT TEST
------------------------

Expo Go validates only foreground movement behavior. Keep Kairo visible while recording.
The readiness screen must say "Expo Go test mode" and explain that background testing needs
the development build.

Permissions and start:

1. Open Movement and Settings.
2. Confirm units, voice cues, distance cues, time cues, and autopause are enabled by default.
3. Toggle every setting off and on; leave them enabled for the first route.
4. Select Run and start tracking.
5. Grant foreground location permission.
6. Confirm Kairo opens the live map and waits for a GPS fix without crashing.

Foreground route:

1. Walk outdoors for at least 300 meters with Kairo visible.
2. Confirm the route line and marker update.
3. Pan the map; confirm it stays where moved until the recenter button is pressed.
4. Press recenter and confirm the latest position returns to view.
5. Confirm distance increases and moving/elapsed time are separate.
6. For Run/Walk, confirm pace is shown. Repeat a short Ride and confirm speed is shown.
7. Manually pause for at least 20 seconds. Distance must not grow materially while paused.
8. Resume and confirm recording continues without creating a second activity.
9. Stand still long enough for autopause, then move long enough for auto-resume.
10. Confirm the AUTOPAUSED indicator and voice announcements match the state.
11. Finish and confirm the activity appears in Movement history.

History, editing, and replay:

1. Open the completed activity.
2. Confirm start/end markers and the route render.
3. Rename it and change the activity type; save and confirm revision increases.
4. Trim at least one point from the start or end.
5. Confirm raw history remains available, the visible route changes, metrics recompute, and
   revision increases again.
6. Open replay. Test play, pause, restart, 1x/2x/4x/8x, and scrubbing at different widths.
7. Change metric/imperial settings and confirm live/history/replay units update consistently.
8. Restart Expo Go and confirm completed history and edits remain.

Voice cues:

1. Use a route or simulator long enough to cross a time or distance threshold.
2. Confirm cues do not repeat after navigating away and returning.
3. Disable distance cues; confirm time cues can still speak.
4. Disable time cues; confirm distance cues can still speak.
5. Disable voice cues globally; confirm no movement speech occurs.


6. ANDROID DEVELOPMENT BUILD - REQUIRED
---------------------------------------

Expo Go cannot pass this section. Build/install a development build using the project's Expo
native configuration. If EAS is configured, use the repository's development profile. If it
is not configured, generate/run the Android native project with the installed Android SDK.
Record the exact build command and artifact version in the result report.

Required Android checks:

1. Launch Kairo and grant foreground location.
2. Grant background/"Allow all the time" location when Android presents that flow.
3. Start a Run and confirm Android shows Kairo's persistent tracking notification.
4. Record outdoors for at least 10 minutes with:
   - 2 minutes foreground
   - 3 minutes screen locked
   - 3 minutes another app foregrounded
   - 2 minutes Kairo foreground again
5. Confirm one continuous activity, sensible distance, and no large straight-line jump.
6. Confirm manual pause/resume works while returning from background.
7. Confirm autopause/auto-resume works after screen lock.
8. Confirm time/distance speech occurs with the screen locked.
9. Repeat speech through a connected Bluetooth headset/speaker.
10. Deny background permission on a second attempt and confirm Kairo explains the requirement
    without creating an unusable active activity.

Force-kill contract:

1. Start another activity and collect several visible points.
2. Force-stop/swipe away Kairo.
3. Reopen it.
4. Kairo is not required to have collected new points while killed.
5. It must recover the active activity and all points persisted before termination.
6. Resume or finish it without duplicate points/events or a second activity.

Battery observation:

1. Record battery percentage before a representative 30-60 minute route.
2. Record battery percentage after the route.
3. Record whether the phone became unusually warm.
4. Report device, OS, duration, GPS mode, screen-on time, and battery delta.


7. IOS VALIDATION
-----------------

Run the equivalent development build on iOS when a Mac/Xcode signing environment and physical
iPhone are available. Verify When In Use/Always permissions, blue background indicator,
screen-lock recording, map tiles, speech/Bluetooth, recovery, and battery use. An iOS bundle
export proves JavaScript/native configuration compatibility but not background execution.


8. BACKEND SYNC ACCEPTANCE
--------------------------

Only run this section when `EXPO_PUBLIC_API_URL` and `EXPO_PUBLIC_DEVICE_KEY` point to the Kairo
backend.

1. Record offline and finish an activity with network disabled.
2. Confirm the activity remains fully usable locally.
3. Re-enable network and trigger normal app sync.
4. Confirm exactly one movement aggregate is created on the backend.
5. Trigger sync again; the exact replay must succeed without duplication.
6. Trim or rename the activity and sync; confirm the backend moves to the higher revision.
7. Delete the activity and sync; confirm deletion is replay-safe.
8. Authenticate as another user and confirm the activity cannot be listed or opened.
9. Confirm no data is sent to Strava or any third-party fitness service.
